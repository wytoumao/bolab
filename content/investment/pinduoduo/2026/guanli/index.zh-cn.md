1. 2026-03-25：Q4/FY2025 新闻稿
陈磊 Lei Chen，Co-Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer
要点：
过去一年公司坚持 high-quality development / 高质量发展战略。
将继续坚持长期主义。
会把更多资源投入其服务的利益相关方。
语境是进入下一个十年的增长。
可确认短摘：
“strategic focus on high-quality development”
“uphold our long-term philosophy”
“channeling greater resources into the stakeholders we serve”
赵佳臻 Jiazhen Zhao，Co-Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer

要点：
2026 年是公司进入下一个十年的起点。
供应链投资是公司最有信念、最核心的投入方向。
公司会以 all-in mindset 投入显著资源，为整个生态创造长期收益。
可确认短摘：
“2026 marks the beginning of our journey into the next decade”
“supply chain investment is where we will place our greatest conviction”
“with an all-in mindset”
“drive lasting benefits to the entire ecosystem”
刘珺 Jun Liu，VP of Finance
要点：
外部环境和竞争格局快速变化。
公司需要持续探索并投入。
这些投入坚定且长期，并会不可避免影响财务表现。
可确认短摘：
“The external environment and competitive landscape are undergoing rapid
changes.”
“These investments are firm and long-term”
“will inevitably affect our financial performance”

2. 2026-05-27：Q1 2026 新闻稿
陈磊 Lei Chen，Co-Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer
要点：
本季度标志着公司业务、内部流程和组织的“深度转型”开始。
平台企业需要承担更大责任。

公司要为用户、产业和社会整体创造更大价值。
可确认短摘：
“start of deep transformations”
“our business, our internal processes and our organization”
“stepping up to our responsibilities as a platform enterprise”
“create greater value for our users, the industries and the society as a whole”
赵佳臻 Jiazhen Zhao，Co-Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer
要点：
进入下一个十年后，供应链投资成为核心战略优先事项。
公司将投入显著资源建设第一方品牌业务。
目标是为供应链伙伴打开新机会，并持续为消费者创造价值。
可确认短摘：
“supply chain investments will be our core strategic priority”
“commit significant resources”
“building the first-party brand business”
“unlocking new opportunities for our supply chain partners”
“driving exceptional value for our customers”
刘珺 Jun Liu，VP of Finance
要点：
公司坚定投资供应链能力。
这些投入是建设更有韧性、更繁荣平台生态的基石。
公司准备长期投入。
可确认短摘：

“investing resolutely in our supply chain capabilities”
“cornerstone of a resilient and thriving platform ecosystem”
“prepared to invest over the long term”
