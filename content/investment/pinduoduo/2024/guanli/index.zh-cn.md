## 二、主要内容整理

1. 2024-03-20：Q4/FY2023 新闻稿
陈磊：
“2023 represents a pivotal chapter in our corporate history as we transition
towards high-quality development,” said Mr. Lei Chen, Chairman and Co-Chief
Executive Officer of PDD Holdings. “In 2024, we remain dedicated to further
improving consumer experiences, enhancing technology innovations, and generating
positive impacts in our communities.”
赵佳臻：
“In the fourth quarter, we saw growing demand driven by encouraging consumer
sentiment,” said Mr. Jiazhen Zhao, Executive Director and Co-Chief Executive
Officer of PDD Holdings. “We will continue our high-quality development strategy,
stay dedicated to offer great value and exceptional service, and keep building
thriving communities that can benefit all.”
刘珺：
“In 2023, our R&D investment exceeded RMB10 billion for the second consecutive
year, underscoring our dedication to technology and agricultural advancements.”
要点：2023 年是转向高质量发展的关键章节；2024 年继续改善消费者体验、技术创新、社
区正面影响；研发投入连续第二年超过 100 亿元，强调技术和农业。
2. 2024-05-22：Q1 2024 新闻稿
陈磊：
“We are committed to offering a trustworthy shopping environment for our users
around the world.”
赵佳臻：

“This year is critical for us to deepen the execution of our high-quality
development strategy.”
“We will focus our efforts on improving the overall consumer experience,
strengthening our supply chain capabilities, and fostering a healthy platform
ecosystem.”
刘珺：
“Rather than focusing on short-term results, we prioritize long-term value creation
and remain committed to further deepening our investments in the future.”
要点：面向全球用户提供可信赖购物环境；2024 年是深化高质量发展战略的关键年；重点是
消费者体验、供应链能力、健康平台生态；强调长期价值，不追求短期结果。
3. 2024-08-26：Q2 2024 新闻稿
陈磊：
“While encouraged by the solid progress we made in the past few quarters, we see
many challenges ahead.”
“We will invest heavily in the platform’s trust and safety, support high-quality
merchants, and relentlessly improve the merchant ecosystem. We are prepared to
accept short-term sacrifices and potential decline in profitability.”
赵佳臻：
“We are committed to nurturing a healthy and sustainable ecosystem where high-
quality merchants thrive.”
“We will vigorously support high-quality merchants while firmly tackling low-quality
ones.”
刘珺：

“Looking ahead, revenue growth will inevitably face pressure due to intensified
competition and external challenges.”
“Profitability will also likely to be impacted as we continue to invest resolutely.”
要点：管理层明确释放挑战增多、增速和利润承压的信号；高质量发展转向生态治理、平台信
任与安全、高质量商家支持、低质量商家治理；接受短期牺牲和潜在盈利下降。
4. 2024-11-21：Q3 2024 新闻稿
陈磊：
“Over the past quarter, our focus remained on driving the high-quality development
of the platforms.”
“We are committed to investing consistently and patiently in our platform
ecosystem to deliver impactful results over the long run.”
赵佳臻：
“In the third quarter, we stepped up the investments in our platform ecosystem
through merchant support policies and trust and safety updates.”
“High-quality merchants are encouraged to further strengthen their product and
service offerings, thereby fostering a virtuous cycle.”
刘珺：
“Our topline growth further moderated quarter-on-quarter amid intensified
competition and ongoing external challenges.”
要点：延续 Q2 的高质量发展和长期投入框架；生态投入包括商家支持政策、信任与安全升
级；收入增速继续放缓，竞争和外部挑战持续。
