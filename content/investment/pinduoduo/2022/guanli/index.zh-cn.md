## 二、可纳入正文的非财报管理层公开文字

2022-11-29：董事离任公告
管理层：陈磊 Lei Chen，Chairman and CEO；Neil Shen，董事 类型：SEC 6-K 公告中的公开声明 是否电话会议：否
原文摘录：
Mr. Lei Chen, the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of the Company, said: “Neil has been a valuable member of our board. I
am deeply grateful for his contributions to our company and I wish him all the best.”
Mr. Neil Shen said: “After serving on the PDD board and witnessing the Company’s impressive development for four years, I have
decided to step down from the Board to focus on my other interests and engagements.”
整理：这是 2022 年官方 SEC 披露中找到的少数非财报、非电话会管理层直接引语，内容仅涉及 Neil Shen 离任和陈磊代表公司感谢其董事
会贡献。

## 三、股东会材料：有材料，无讲话

2022-07-08：AGM Notice / Proxy Statement
AGM 事项包括重新选举陈磊为董事。Proxy Statement 对陈磊履历说明：
Lei Chen is a founding member of our company and has served as our director and chief executive officer since July 2020. Mr.
Chen was appointed as our chairman of the Board in March 2021.
这属于股东会材料/董事履历，不是管理层讲话。

## 四、边界材料：财报新闻稿 quote（非电话会，但建议仅备查）

2022 年财报新闻稿中的管理层引述主要集中在以下主题：
从销售营销转向研发和农业长期投入。
10 Billion Agriculture Initiative / 百亿农研相关投入。
“Benefit All, People First, More Open”。
投资农业、研发、供应链效率、农业数字普惠。
用户增长放缓、消费复苏、竞争和长期价值创造。
代表摘录：
2022-03-21 陈磊：
“In 2021, we made the strategic shift from sales and marketing toward research and development.”
2022-05-27 陈磊：
“We remain focused on making long-term investments in agriculture and abide by our philosophy of ‘Benefit All’, ‘People
First’, and ‘More Open’.”
2022-08-29 陈磊：
“We remain committed to building a platform that serves as a force for good.”
2022-11-28 陈磊：

“We will increase our R&D investment to further enhance the supply chain efficiency and agricultural digital inclusion.”
