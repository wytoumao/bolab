## 二、核心非财报材料

1. 2023-04-04：任命赵佳臻为联席 CEO 及执行董事
管理层：陈磊 Lei Chen；赵佳臻 Jiazhen Zhao 类型：高管任命公告/管理层声明 是否电话会议：否

原文摘录：
“Jiazhen and I have established deep trust after working together for many years,” said Mr. Lei Chen, Chairman of the Board
and Co-Chief Executive Officer of the Company. “His new leadership role reflects that our team is full of vitality and our next
generation of leaders are ready to take on more challenges. In the future, Jiazhen will concentrate more on supply chain
management and China operations, while my focus will be more on globalization efforts.”
“I am honored that our Board has put its trust in me,” said Mr. Jiazhen Zhao, Executive Director and Co-Chief Executive Officer
of the Company. “I will continue to work closely with Lei, and adhere to our principles of ‘Benefit All’, ‘People First’, and ‘More
Open’ to create value for consumers, our ecosystem partners, and the society.”
整理要点：
赵佳臻成为联席 CEO 和执行董事。
陈磊称二人多年共事建立深厚信任，新任命说明团队充满活力，下一代领导者准备承担更多挑战。
分工：赵佳臻聚焦供应链管理和中国业务；陈磊聚焦全球化。
赵佳臻强调坚持 Benefit All、People First、More Open，为消费者、生态伙伴和社会创造价值。
2. 2023-11-28：齐鲁辞任董事公告
管理层/董事：齐鲁 Qi Lu；陈磊 Lei Chen 类型：董事辞任公告/公开声明 是否电话会议：否
原文摘录：
Dr. Qi Lu said: “During my time as director, I have had the opportunity to witness the Company’s impressive development. I am
proud of what we as an organization have accomplished over the past many years. In order to spend more time needed for my
other work, I have made the difficult decision to resign from the Board.”
Mr. Lei Chen, the Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer of the Company, said: “We and our Board greatly appreciate Dr.
Lu’s service and contributions to our Company. I wish him all the best in his future endeavors.”
整理：齐鲁因需投入更多时间于其他工作辞任董事；陈磊代表公司和董事会感谢其贡献。
3. 2023-08-31：General Counsel transition
公告称朱健翀不再担任 General Counsel，General Counsel 职能由 Legal and Compliance Committee 承担，该委员会由陈磊主持。无管
理层直接 quote，作为治理背景备查。
